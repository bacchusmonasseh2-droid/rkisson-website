# Photographs, branding and documents to request from the company

## Branding
- Official logo in vector format (AI, EPS, SVG or PDF), plus any white/reversed version. The site currently uses a plain typographic stand-in (`src/components/Logo.astro`).
- Brand colours and fonts, if defined.
- Letterhead, business card, vehicle or signboard artwork (useful references even if informal).

## Project photographs (highest priority)
Genuine photographs replace nothing on the site today, they add to it: project cards have an empty photo slot.
- Completed water systems: Kamarang, Jawalla, Monkey Mountain, Taruka, Kamana, Sand Hill (well head, trestle and tanks, solar array, standpipes).
- Drilling in progress: rig on site, casing, pump testing.
- Pipe laying: trenching, pipes strung out, jointing.
- Lethem housing scheme: roads, drains, culverts, before and after.
- Buildings: CDC warehouse and Immigration Office at Lethem, Mazaruni quarters.
- UG Early Childhood Centre bridge.
- Any sea defence, drainage or irrigation work.
- Commissioning or handover ceremonies (with permission of those pictured).

For each photo: project name, location, approximate date, and confirmation the company owns it or has permission to use it. Original files from the phone or camera are best.

## Equipment
- Photograph of each machine offered for rental, with make, model and key specifications.

## People and premises
- Photograph of the office or yard at Foulis.
- Team photographs on site, and a portrait of the principal if a leadership section is wanted.

## Documents
- Company profile or capability statement, if one exists.
- Business registration and current compliance certificates (GRA, NIS).
- Local Content Register certificate and GCCI membership confirmation, with validity dates.
- List of completed projects with client, value and completion date; completion certificates or reference letters.
- Health and safety policy, if any.
- Any client testimonials, with written permission to publish.
