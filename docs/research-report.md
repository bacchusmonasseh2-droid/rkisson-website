# Research report — R. Kissoon Contracting Service

Prepared November 2026 from publicly available sources. Amounts are in Guyana dollars (G$).

## 1. How to read this report

Every statement is tagged with the strength of the evidence behind it.

| Tag | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Commissioned** | A government release or news report states the works were commissioned / handed over and names the company as contractor. |
| **In progress (as reported)** | A dated public report describes the works as under way. Present status unknown. |
| **Awarded** | A contract award or signing is on record. No evidence of completion was found. |
| **Bid only** | The company appears as a bidder in tender-opening records. Not evidence of an award. |
| **Advertised** | The company lists this as a service. No project record was found. |
| **Unverified** | Supplied in the brief or seen only in a search snippet; could not be checked against the source. |

## 2. Company profile

- **Name:** R. Kissoon Contracting Service. Public sources also print "R. Kissoon Contracting Services", "Kissoon Contracting Services", "R Kissoon Contracting" and, once, "R. Kisson". Two further variants, "R. Kissoon and Son Contracting Services" (2019) and "R. Kissoon Construction" (2024), may or may not be the same business.
- **Principal:** A Ministry of Housing and Water item names **Romesh Kissoon** as receiving a contract on behalf of the company. News reports refer to an unnamed "Managing Director". No title has been confirmed.
- **Base:** Foulis Housing Scheme, East Coast Demerara. The brief gives Lot 687, 18th Street; an older telephone directory listing shows "678 17th Street Foulis". One of these needs correcting.
- **History:** No founding date, incorporation record or ownership information was found. The earliest contract award located is from **July 2016**. The website therefore says only that public records show the company carrying out government contracts "since at least 2016".
- **Clients on record:** Guyana Water Inc. (by far the largest), Central Housing and Planning Authority, Basic Needs Trust Fund, Civil Defence Commission, Ministry of Home Affairs, Ministry of Public Security / Guyana Prison Service.
- **Geography on record:** Regions 3, 4, 7, 8 and 9, with a clear concentration in hinterland Regions 7, 8 and 9.
- **Procurement profile:** GuyanaProcurement lists 13 awards between 15 Nov 2022 and 24 Dec 2025, all by national competitive bidding, 12 of them from Guyana Water Inc., with a published total of G$547.9 million. (Several rows in that table are visibly duplicated, so the true count and total are lower; see section 4.)

## 5. Matters the company should be aware of

These are on the public record and were deliberately **not** used on the website. They are listed so the company is not surprised by them.

- **June 2022:** News Room Guyana reported a dispute between Guyana Water Inc. and the company involving a claim/counterclaim of about G$19 million. Outcome not found.
- **January 2024:** Kaieteur News reported an Opposition MP's statement that parts of Jawalla and Kamarang, including the hospital, were not receiving adequate water. The report concerns service levels and does not attribute fault to the contractor.
- A business-directory aggregator shows a 3.3-star rating from 6 reviews; the entry looks machine-generated.

## 6. Branding and photographs

- No official logo, brand colours or company photographs could be retrieved. The website uses a plain typographic wordmark as a stand-in, clearly marked in the code for replacement.
- No company social media page was found. Social links are therefore not shown.
- Press coverage of the commissioning ceremonies includes photographs of the completed systems. These belong to the publishers and were not used. The company's own photographs of those sites would be the most valuable addition to the website.
