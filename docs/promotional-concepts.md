# Promotional visual concepts

Shared rules: navy `#0a1d38`, yellow `#f5b800`, concrete `#f5f5f2`; Archivo (wide, bold) for headlines, IBM Plex Sans for text, IBM Plex Mono for small labels. A yellow rule and corner "survey ticks" tie the material to the website. Add all text in a design tool, never inside the generated image. State only verified facts. Illustrative images carry a small "Illustrative image" note; announcements of real projects use real photographs.

## 1. Social media posts (1:1 and 4:5)

- **"What we build" series, six posts.** One per service. Full-bleed service image, dark gradient at the bottom, mono label "SERVICE 01", headline "Water Infrastructure", one line of scope, phone number.
- **"From the record" series.** Navy card, no photo needed. Large figure and one fact, with the source named: "150 m — depth of the well drilled for Taruka's first water system. Commissioned February 2023." Others: "700 people — Kamarang's first potable water supply, November 2021"; "6 communities with water systems commissioned, Regions 7 and 8."
- **"How it works" carousel.** Five slides following the delivery steps on the About page.
- **Enquiry prompt.** Yellow card: "Planning a well, a road or a building? Request a quotation." Phone and email.

## 2. Company introduction graphics

- **Profile banner** (Facebook 1640×624, LinkedIn 1584×396): hero road image, navy fade from the left, wordmark, "Building With Integrity", six service names in a single mono line.
- **One-slide introduction** (16:9): left half navy with name, slogan and a three-line description; right half the trestle image; bottom strip styled as a drawing title block with address, phones and email.
- **Email signature strip** (600×120): wordmark, slogan, contact line.

## 3. Service advertisements

- **Water wells and supply systems** (press / online, 4:5): well-drilling image; headline "Clean water, from borehole to household tap."; three ticks: well drilling and development, transmission and distribution mains, complete community systems; call to action "Request a quotation".
- **Roads, drainage and bridges:** laterite road image; headline "Infrastructure that opens up communities."
- **Building construction:** building image; headline "New buildings and rehabilitation."
- **Equipment rental:** equipment image; headline "Need machinery for a job? Ask us what is available." No machine list until the inventory is confirmed.
- Sea defence and drainage/irrigation adverts should say what the service is and invite enquiries, without implying past projects.

## 4. Project announcements

Template for use **only** with a confirmed milestone and a genuine photograph.

- Layout: real photo on top two-thirds; navy panel below with status label (CONTRACT SIGNED / WORKS UNDER WAY / COMMISSIONED), project name, location and region, client, one sentence of scope.
- Example text: "COMMISSIONED · Kamana and Sand Hill Water Supply Systems, Region 8 · Client: Guyana Water Inc. · Deep well, intake and pumping chamber, pipe networks and 70 service connections."
- Never use an illustrative image in this template.

## 5. Corporate flyers (A4 / Letter, two sides)

- **Front:** hero image top half; wordmark and slogan; three-sentence company description; six services in a two-column grid with numbers 01–06.
- **Back:** "Documented experience" list of the commissioned water systems and the Lethem infrastructure contract, each with client and date; "Clients" line (Guyana Water Inc., Central Housing and Planning Authority, Basic Needs Trust Fund, government agencies); contact block; space for the official logo and any confirmed registrations.
- **Tender insert variant:** single-sided capability sheet with the same content in monochrome navy for inclusion in bid submissions.
